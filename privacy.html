Privacy Policy

Your data protection is our priority

Last Updated: January 22, 2026

TriviaVault commits to safeguarding your privacy. This policy details our information collection, utilization, disclosure, and protection practices.

1. Information Collection

Personal Data

We collect: contact information (email, phone), account credentials, payment details, and demographic data.

Usage Data

Automatically collected: challenge participation, performance metrics, device information, IP addresses, and cookie data.

2. Information Utilization

Data serves platform operations (account management, scoring, point tracking, withdrawal processing), communication (notifications, support responses), improvement analysis, and legal compliance.

3. Data Security

Local Storage

Browser localStorage maintains point balances and preferences on your device, transmitting only during withdrawals.

Security Protocols

Implementation includes SSL/TLS encryption, secure password hashing, regular audits, access controls, and firewall protection.

Retention Policy

Personal information retained during active account status. Inactive accounts (12+ months) may face deletion.

4. Information Sharing

We Do NOT Sell Your Data

TriviaVault never sells, rents, or trades personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Sharing Scenarios

Limited sharing with: service providers (payment processors, hosting), legal requirements, business transfers, or with explicit consent.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Essential cookies enable functionality. Performance cookies analyze usage. Preference cookies remember settings. Browser settings control cookie management (disabling may affect functionality).

6. Your Privacy Rights

Rights include: data access and portability, correction and updates, deletion requests, marketing opt-out, cookie management, and consent withdrawal.

Exercise rights by contacting hello@triviavault.com (30-day response timeframe).

7. Children's Privacy

Platform intended for 13+ users. We don't knowingly collect data from under-13 individuals. Parental discovery of such collection should trigger immediate contact for deletion.

8. Data Breach Protocol

Personal information breaches trigger notification to affected users and relevant authorities within 72 hours per applicable regulations.

9. Policy Modifications

Updates posted with revised dates. Significant changes communicated via email/notification. Continued usage constitutes updated policy acceptance.
